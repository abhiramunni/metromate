Ride smart

Six things regulars know

01

QR beats the queue

Buy a QR ticket in the Namma Metro app or at the counter — same 5% discount as a smart card, no card to carry.

02

Off-peak is 10% off

Travel before 8 AM or between 12–4 PM with a smart card or QR and the discount doubles to 10%.

03

Mind the 120 minutes

Finish your journey within 2 hours of entering, or a ₹50-per-hour overstay charge applies.

04

Change at Majestic or RV Road

Only two live interchanges: Majestic (Purple ↔ Green) and RV Road (Green ↔ Yellow). Silk Board joins when the Blue Line opens.

05

Pack light

Big luggage is not allowed on trains. Keep bags small and use the lift if you are carrying anything heavy.

06

Rain changes everything

On wet evenings, leave 15 minutes early — platforms get crowded and last-mile autos get scarce.
